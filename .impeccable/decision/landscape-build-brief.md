# Surface: landscape-build.html

Mode: Persuade
Audience: private clients who chose landscape implementation as a related direction for a cottage.
Job: understand what turnkey landscape works include, see garden stills as character, and leave a request.
Action: call, write, or submit the contacts form.
Proof: published landscape implementation scope from the site; existing garden stills, not claimed as completed YMG jobs.
Constraints: inherit landscape.html inner-page pattern, section-head H2, flex layouts, no invented claims, no portfolio CTA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Landscape implementation is a calm service page in the same family as landscape.html, not a homepage clone and not a grid of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black cool ground, Cormorant display, Manrope body, orange #ef8535, glass header, garden stills, shared page-hero and section-head.

STORY: The visitor understands that landscape works can continue the same cottage project, what the work includes, and how to start.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header. Full-bleed garden still. Centered h1 «Реализация на участке», one supporting line. Scroll to letter, stills, scope, path, then the form.

FORM: Extension of landscape.html inner-page pattern (code-led).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
