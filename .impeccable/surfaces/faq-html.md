# Surface: faq.html

Mode: Read
Audience: private clients comparing interior studios before a first call.
Job: answer the recurring decision questions about cost, scope, timeline, and format without inventing prices.
Action: continue to contacts or call.
Proof: honest process answers only. No fake ₽/m², awards, or team bios.
Constraints: inherit landing visual world and about/contacts page-hero pattern.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FAQ opens as a calm reading surface with one still and a researched stack of cottage-interior questions, not a sales quiz or price table.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, Cormorant display, Manrope body, orange #ef8535 actions, glass header, real project photo under a dark veil, shared page-hero and accordion FAQ components.

STORY: The visitor learns what a design project includes, how pricing is discussed, whether design-only is possible, and how to start a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header. Full-bleed portfolio still with dark overlay. Centered h1 «Вопросы / и ответы», one supporting line. Scroll to a short intro, then the accordion and a contacts CTA.

FORM: Extension of the about/contacts inner-page pattern (code-led, narrowly specified).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
