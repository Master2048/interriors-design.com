# Surface: services.html

Mode: Persuade
Audience: private clients choosing which direction to start with.
Job: scan six service directions with real photos and open the matching detail page.
Action: click a service tile toward a dedicated service page (or leave a request later).
Proof: existing service photography and published service scopes only.
Constraints: inherit landing visual world and inner-page hero pattern; interior-first order.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Services open as a calm hub of six photo-led directions, not a sticky stacked scrollytelling clone of the homepage.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, Cormorant display, Manrope body, orange #ef8535 actions, glass header, real service photos, shared page-hero type, flex tile grid.

STORY: The visitor sees that interior design leads, related directions continue the same house, and each tile opens a deeper page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header. Full-bleed interior service still with dark overlay. Centered h1 «Услуги / YMG DESIGN», one supporting line. Scroll to short intro, then a two-column flex grid of service tiles.

FORM: Extension of the about/contacts/faq inner-page pattern (code-led, narrowly specified).
