# Surface: interior.html

Mode: Persuade
Audience: private clients who chose interior design as the starting direction.
Job: understand what the interior project includes, see real stills, and leave a request.
Action: call, write, or submit the contacts form.
Proof: published interior scope and existing portfolio stills only.
Constraints: inherit inner-page hero, section-head H2 pattern, flex layouts, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Interior design is a calm, readable service page, not a homepage clone and not a grid of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black cool ground, Cormorant display, Manrope body, orange #ef8535, glass header, real interior photographs, shared page-hero and section-head.

STORY: The visitor understands that cottage interiors are the studio's main work, what the project contains, and how to start.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header. Full-bleed interior still. Centered h1 «Дизайн интерьера», one supporting line. Scroll to letter, scope, path, stills, then the form.

FORM: Extension of about/contacts/faq/services inner-page pattern (code-led).
