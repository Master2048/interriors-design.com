# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Static multi-page website. The production pages load minified CSS and JavaScript assets with cache-busted versions.

## Users

Primary users are private clients in Saint Petersburg and the Leningrad region who want an interior for a private house or cottage, with architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape available as related directions. They evaluate the studio from a phone or laptop, then leave a request or call.

Secondary audience: returning clients and people comparing a design studio with a full-cycle team.

## Product Purpose

YMG DESIGN primarily creates interiors for private houses and cottages. Architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape are related directions that can continue the same project. The public site explains the studio and its services, shows completed project stories, answers common questions, and starts a conversation through a form, phone, or email.

Success on this surface: the visitor understands the studio's role and full-cycle capabilities, finds a relevant service or project, trusts the work enough to inquire, and can complete that inquiry on any device, including keyboard-only.

## Positioning

The primary offer is interior design for private houses and cottages. When needed, the studio continues the project through architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape, keeping the home coherent from the first idea to the final detail.

## Operating Context

The visitor arrives cold from search or referral, often on a phone. The main page introduces the studio and leads through services, process, portfolio, FAQ, and contacts. Inner pages provide dedicated content for the studio, interior design, architecture, construction, finishing, landscape design, landscape implementation, portfolio, six project stories, FAQ, contacts, and privacy. Working hours, phones, email, and social links are published in the contact areas. A privacy policy explains how request data is handled.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed on the current site:

- Static multi-page marketing site with `index.html`, dedicated service pages, `about.html`, `portfolio.html`, six project pages, `faq.html`, `contacts.html`, and `privacy.html`.
- Inquiry form on contact surfaces and a quick-request modal with required name, phone, and privacy consent, plus optional email and message.
- Direct phone, email, Instagram, VK, and Telegram links.
- Responsive mobile navigation with a services accordion, FAQ accordion, reveal animations, quick CTA, back-to-top control, and project portfolio interactions.
- Project galleries and portfolio cards with real project photography.
- Hero and visualization videos where used; motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Source CSS and JavaScript are built into minified production assets and served with cache-busted versions.

Undecided / not claimed here: legal entity details beyond the studio name, pricing packages as a contract, CMS or backend, authenticated accounts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **YMG DESIGN**.
- Voice: Russian, calm and concrete, first-person plural (“создаём интерьер”, “проектируем и строим”), no invented testimonials or inflated promises.
- Identity: letter mark **Y**, wordmark **YMG DESIGN**, line “студия дизайна”.
- Contact facts published on the site: `+7 (931) 235 02 02`, `8 (964) 336 42 48`, `info@ymg-design.com`.
- Copy avoids vague phrases about “образ жизни” and “сценарии жизни”, as well as the formal word “столовая”. Prefer wording about tasks, functions, requirements, the kitchen, dining area, and communication space.

Visual world (palette, type, motion) is not specified here; it lives in the incumbent CSS and, if written later, DESIGN.md.

## Product Principles

1. Interior design is the primary offer; architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape can continue the same project.
2. Proof is the work itself (photos, video), not slogans or fake reviews.
3. Starting a conversation is the conversion; phones and the form must stay reachable.
4. Calm, dark, material craft over loud marketing chrome.
5. Every service and project page should explain its subject directly, without decorative claims replacing useful information.
6. The site must remain usable on a phone, with a keyboard, and with reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No contractual WCAG level is published. For this public marketing site, keyboard access, visible focus, contrast for body copy, and reduced-motion alternatives are treated as required so a client can reach the form.
