# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are private clients in Russia who want an interior for a comfort-class cottage, with architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape available as related directions. They evaluate the studio from a phone or laptop, then leave a request or call.

Secondary audience: returning clients and people comparing a design-only studio versus a full-cycle builder.

## Product Purpose

YMG DESIGN primarily creates interiors for private cottages. Architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape are related directions that can continue the same project. The public site exists so a prospective client can understand this approach, see the work, and start a conversation (form, phone, or email) without first visiting an office.

Success on this surface: the visitor understands that one studio can take a house from idea to move-in, trusts the work enough to inquire, and can actually complete that inquiry on any device, including keyboard-only.

## Positioning

The site’s own claim is interiors in which the client’s life feels natural. When needed, the studio continues the project through architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape, keeping the home coherent from the first idea to the final detail.

## Operating Context

The visitor arrives cold from search or referral, often on a phone. They scan a cinematic first screen, services, a project timeline, portfolio, FAQ, then contacts. Working hours, phones, and Instagram sit on the contacts block. A privacy policy explains how request data is handled.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed on the current site:

- Static marketing landing (`index.html`) plus `privacy.html`.
- Inquiry form (name, phone, optional email and message, privacy consent).
- Direct tel/mailto/Instagram links.
- Portfolio lightbox, mobile navigation with a services accordion, FAQ accordion.
- Hero and visualization videos; motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Undecided / not claimed here: legal entity details beyond the studio name, pricing packages as a contract, CMS or backend, authenticated accounts.

## Product Principles

1. Interior design is the primary offer; architecture, construction, finishing, and landscape can continue the same project.
2. Proof is the work itself (photos, video), not slogans or fake reviews.
3. Starting a conversation is the conversion; phones and the form must stay reachable.
4. Calm, dark, material craft over loud marketing chrome.
5. The landing must remain usable on a phone, with a keyboard, and with reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No contractual WCAG level is published. For this public marketing site, keyboard access, visible focus, contrast for body copy, and reduced-motion alternatives are treated as required so a client can reach the form.
